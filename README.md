Developmental Biologist doing Neuroscience. My main interest is studying how the nervous system generates cell types at the right time, in the right place, in the right proportions, to generate functional circuits. For this, I study the spinal cord of zebrafish and use old-school and cutting-edge new techniques that span lineage tracing, transcriptomics and molecular biology.
